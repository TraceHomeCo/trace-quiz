Question 01 — Air Quality

Do you run a HEPA air purifier in your home?

Indoor air typically contains 5–10x more pollutants than outdoor air. A true HEPA filter captures particles down to 0.3 microns — including dust, pet dander, VOCs, and fine particulate matter.

Question 02 — Water Quality

What do you use for drinking water?

Tap water in most cities is technically safe but often contains trace chlorine, fluoride, lead (from older pipes), and microplastics. The type of filter matters significantly.

Question 03 — Daily Skin Contact

What laundry detergent do you use?

You're in contact with your sheets and clothing for 8–16 hours a day. Residual detergent stays in fabric after washing and absorbs directly through your skin — one of the highest daily chemical exposures in most homes.

Question 04 — Food Contact

What cookware do you use most often?

When non-stick coatings are scratched or overheated above 500°F, they release PFAS compounds — a class of "forever chemicals" linked to hormone disruption, immune suppression, and cancer risk. This exposure goes directly into food.

Question 05 — Airborne Chemical Load

Do you use synthetic fragrance products in your home?

The word "fragrance" on a label can legally conceal up to 3,500 chemicals — most of them untested. Plug-in air fresheners, scented candles, and dryer sheets are among the highest sources of indoor VOCs and phthalates.
